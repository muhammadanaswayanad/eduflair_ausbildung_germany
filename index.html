Common questions

I don’t know German. Can I still apply? +

Yes. Start A1–B2 in Calicut. After B2, the same team processes your student course and visa.

I already have B2. Do I still need classes? +

Usually no. Pick “Already certified.” We verify Goethe, TELC, OSD, or similar, then process.

How long until I reach Germany? +

From scratch: about 8 months of German, then ~6 months processing, plus embassy time. Already B2? Skip the language block.

Worker or student? +

Student. You train on a stipend. A full-time role typically comes after you finish the course.

Is a blocked account required? +

Not for Ausbildung. The stipend is meant to cover living costs.
